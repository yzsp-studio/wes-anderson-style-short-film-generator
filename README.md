## 🇨🇳 中文

根据确认的简报与参考素材，制作原创的对称构图叙事短片、品牌短片或社交视频：中心构图、刻意对称、童话式取景、精确调度、克制的复古制作设计与干冷奇想。流程分六步——确认简报、分析素材、锁定制作规格、设计视觉方向、建立脚本与元素及关键帧预览、批量生成分镜组，并在高成本视频生成与最终合成前暂停等确认。片名或导演名只作为路由提示，生成时必须转译为原创的高层视觉属性，不复制具体电影的镜头、角色、对白或画面，也不复制在世创作者的个人签名。默认 16:9，只在用户明确要求竖屏社交交付时用 9:16；章节默认不超过四章。不适用于普通剪辑或电影复刻。

- 简报一次问全：片名、时长、画幅、语言、旁白、章节偏好

- 关键帧预览 + 连续性锚点：重复元素、道具、主体数量
- 分镜组按章节批量生成，逐镜写明节拍、构图、运镜、声音与时长

## 🇬🇧 English

Works from a confirmed brief and reference material to make original symmetric-composition narrative shorts, brand films or social videos: centred framing, deliberate symmetry, storybook staging, precise blocking, restrained retro production design and dry whimsy. Six steps run in order — confirm the brief, analyse the material, lock production specs, design the visual direction, build script, elements and keyframe previews, then batch-generate the storyboard set — pausing for approval before expensive video generation and final compositing. A film title or director name is only a routing hint and must be translated into original high-level visual attributes: no copying of specific shots, characters, dialogue or frames, and no imitation of a living creator's signature. Default is 16:9, switching to 9:16 only for an explicitly requested vertical social cut, with four chapters or fewer. Not for ordinary editing or film replication.

### ✨ What it does

- One intake covers title, runtime, aspect ratio, language, narration and chapter preference
- Semantic material analysis: characters, scenes, era, recurring motifs, symmetry opportunities
- Final_Video_Spec locks specs and the original visual grammar
- Visual direction: composition rules, material and colour relationships, props, wardrobe, per-chapter tone
- Keyframe previews plus continuity anchors: recurring elements, props, subject counts
- Storyboard sets batch-generated per chapter, each shot stating beat, composition, camera intent, sound and duration
